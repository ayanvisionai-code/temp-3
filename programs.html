Private Goaltender Training

One-on-one development focused on the specific needs of each goaltender. Our senior coaching staff breaks down micro-mechanics, movement pathways, and game-situation reads.

Movement: Efficient crease shuffles, T-pushes, and edge control.
Balance & Posture: Center of gravity stability for continuous recovery.
Positioning & Depth: Square angles and post integration (RVH mechanics).

The World Pro Standard

Efficiency. Control.
Balance. Decision Making.

Goaltending is a discipline of millimeters and timing. Our methodology is built around foundational game-situation movements, high repetition, and eliminating unnecessary energy waste.

High Repetition

Muscular and neurological memory is built through focused, quality repetitions in realistic crease angles.

Compact Butterfly

Sealing the ice without creating holes in the five-hole or upper corners, maintaining tight hand positioning.

Read-and-React

Developing puck tracking from stick blade to body, training goalies to react to real cues rather than guessing early.

Crease Patience

Holding the stance, waiting out shooters on breakaways, and allowing the shooter to make the first move.
